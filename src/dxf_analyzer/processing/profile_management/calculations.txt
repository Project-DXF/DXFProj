-Definitions of parameters.
1. Compactness (C)
🔹 Formula:
C=P/24πA 
Where:
P = Perimeter of the shape
A = Area of the shape

🧠 What It Measures:
How circular or irregular a shape is
Low C ≈ Circle-like (most compact)
High C ≈ Irregular or elongated shapes

💡 Engineering Meaning:
A mandrel with high compactness may have long narrow paths, sharp corners, or lobes → harder to extrude, risk of unbalanced flow.
Higher compactness → more complexity → potential die correction or scrap risk.
2.  Solidity
🔹 Formula:
Solidity=Area/Convex Hull Area ​ 
Where:
Convex Hull is the smallest convex shape that wraps the object (like a rubber band around it)

🧠 What It Measures:
How filled in the shape is
Detects concavities, holes, or deep indents

💡 Engineering Meaning:
A mandrel with low solidity has gaps or notches, leading to uneven material flow or thin wall sections.
Important for detecting sharp cavities, which are hard to fill uniformly.
3. Complexity Index C₁:
C1=Ps/As
Where:
Ps: Perimeter of the extruded section (Sum of perimeter of outer loop and inner loops)
As​: Area of the extruded section
4. Complexity Index C₂:
C2=Ps/Ws 
Where:
Ps: Perimeter of the extruded section (Sum of perimeter of outer loop and inner loops)
Ws​: Weight per unit length
5. Complexity Index C₃ (Form Factor):
C3=CCD/Tm 
Where:
CCD: Circumscribing circle diameter
Tm ​: Minimum wall thickness
6. Complexity Index C₄ (Groover's Definition):
C4=0.98+0.02(Ps/P0)^2.25 
Where:
Ps: Perimeter of the extruded section (Sum of perimeter of outer loop and inner loops)
P0​: Perimeter of a round shape with the same cross-sectional area as the profile
7. Complexity Index C₅ (Qamar’s Definition):
C5=0.95+0.05(Ps/P0)^1.5 
Ps: Perimeter of the extruded section (Sum of perimeter of outer loop and inner loops)
P0​: Perimeter of a round shape with the same cross-sectional area as the profile
